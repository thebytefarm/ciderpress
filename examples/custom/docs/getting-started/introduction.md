# Welcome to Acme Corp

Acme Corp is the operating layer for ambitious internet companies. Ship
faster, integrate deeper, and stay accountable to your customers — all
from one platform.

## Why Acme

The teams shipping fastest in 2026 share one trait: they treat their
stack as a single coordinated product, not a federation of tools.
Acme is built around that idea.

- **One graph.** Customers, events, billing, and entitlements share a
  unified resource model.
- **One contract.** Strong TypeScript types from the database through
  the edge — no per-layer DTOs.
- **One control plane.** Feature flags, audit logs, rate limits, and
  policy live next to your code, not behind a vendor portal.

## What you get out of the box

- A typed SDK published to your private npm registry on every push.
- An OpenAPI spec generated from your handlers — never out of sync.
- A managed edge runtime with regional pinning and zero-config DR.
- A dashboard with audit logs, traces, and customer impersonation.

## Who uses Acme

We serve infrastructure-led teams between 10 and 5,000 engineers.
Production deployments range from solo founders shipping their first
SaaS to platform teams running 40,000 services.
